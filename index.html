About me

The person behind
the words.

A personal record of small things noticed.

Hello, I'm Anna.

I am a lawyer, a writer at heart, and a photographer when the light is right.

I did my LL.M in Intellectual Property Law at the University of Edinburgh, which is also, as it happens, where I live now. Law taught me to pay close attention to the details that other people walk past, because the detail you overlook is usually the one that matters most.

This blog is what happens when I cannot stop noticing things. The small, unremarkable scenes that most people pass through without a second thought. The moment just before something happens, and the strange, involuntary way the chest responds.

I grew up in India and moved to Edinburgh for the LL.M. Edinburgh has my heart.

I travel when I can, photograph what stops me, and write about what I cannot let go of. All three are, at their core, the same act.

If you have found your way here, I imagine you are the same kind of person. Or I might have shared this with you.
